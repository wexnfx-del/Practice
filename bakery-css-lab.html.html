About us

Juniper & Grain is a neighborhood bakery built around simple ingredients and slow mornings. Every loaf starts the night before, when the starter gets fed nd the dough gets its first long rest

We bake in small batches, so when the day's bread is gone, it's gone. Come early, or call ahead and we'll set a loaf aside with your name on it
